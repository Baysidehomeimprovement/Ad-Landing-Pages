What shapes your estimate

Every renovation is priced from your actual rooms and selections, not a square-foot average. These three things move the number most, and we walk through all of them before you sign anything.

SCOPE

Which rooms, and how many

A kitchen and two baths in one project costs less per room than three separate jobs, because demolition, permits, trades and dust containment are shared. Phasing is an option if it fits your budget better.

SELECTIONS

Finish and material level

Cabinetry, tile, quartz and fixtures span a wide range. We show you the range at design stage, so you decide where to spend and where to save before the price locks.

STRUCTURE

Whether walls or plumbing move

Cosmetic updates stay predictable. Opening a wall, relocating plumbing or changing a layout brings in structural review and permits, which we handle but which do change the number.

You get a fixed, itemized estimate in writing before any demolition begins, and it is the number you pay unless you change the scope. $0 down financing is available, and your designer can review options during the consultation.
